How Our Age Calculation Logic Works

Calculating age seems simple on the surface, but the math behind the scenes is surprisingly complex due to the irregularities of the Gregorian calendar. Our tool utilizes a precise algorithm that accounts for:

  • Leap Year Compensation: Not every year has 365 days. By accounting for February 29th every four years, we ensure that your total day count is 100% accurate.
  • Varying Month Lengths: Since months range from 28 to 31 days, our tool precisely calculates the "remainder" days when moving between your birth date and the current date.
  • Date of Birth (DOB) Relativity: We allow you to set a custom "Calculate at Date," which is perfect for historical research or planning for future retirement milestones.
