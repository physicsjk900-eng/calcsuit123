Order of Operations

Always remember the **PEMDAS** rule (Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication & Division from left to right, Addition & Subtraction from left to right). While this basic tool handles one operation at a time, keeping this logic in mind ensures mathematical accuracy in your complex projects.

Fun Fact

Understanding the Four Fundamental Operations

The core of any basic calculator revolves around the "Big Four" of arithmetic. Mastery of these is essential for more advanced concepts like algebra and calculus.

Addition (+)

The process of calculating the total of two or more numbers. Essential for budgeting and inventory.

Subtraction (-)

Finding the difference between numbers. Crucial for determining profit margins or remaining time.

Multiplication (×)

Scaling a number by another number. Used for calculating area, tax totals, and bulk pricing.

Division (÷)

Splitting a large number into equal parts. Perfect for unit pricing and sharing costs among groups.
