Q: What is the mid-market rate?

A: The mid-market rate is the real-time midpoint between the global buy and sell rates, representing the fairest market value.

Q: Why do banks charge more than the converter shows?

A: Banks and conversion services add a markup (retail spread) or service fee to the mid-market exchange rate to cover operational costs and generate profit.
