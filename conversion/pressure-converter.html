Q: What is 1 atmosphere (atm) of pressure?

A: 1 atm represents the average atmospheric pressure at sea level, equal to 101.325 kPa or 14.696 PSI.

Q: How does elevation affect atmospheric pressure?

A: As elevation increases, atmospheric pressure decreases because there is less air mass above pushing down.
