Q: What is a knot?

A: A knot is a unit of speed equal to one nautical mile per hour, or exactly 1.852 km/h (about 1.151 mph).

Q: What is the speed of sound in air?

A: At sea level and 20°C, the speed of sound is approximately 343 m/s, 1,235 km/h, or 767 mph.
