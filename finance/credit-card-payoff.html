Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Credit Card Payoff:

Payoff timeline is calculated using the following amortization equation:
\(N = -\frac{\log(1 - \frac{r \cdot PV}{PMT})}{\log(1 + r)}\)
Where \(N\) is months, \(PV\) is the card balance, \(PMT\) is the monthly payment, and \(r\) is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12).
