Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate DRIP Growth:

Compounding with dividend reinvestment is calculated iteratively:
\(\text{Shares}_{t+1} = \text{Shares}_t + \frac{\text{Shares}_t \times \text{Dividend Per Share}}{\text{Stock Price}}\)
This formula accounts for stock appreciation and dividend growth factors over time.

Q: What is a DRIP?

A: A Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP) is an option offered by companies that automatically uses cash dividends to buy more shares of the stock.

Q: Do I pay taxes on reinvested dividends?

A: Yes, reinvested dividends are typically treated as taxable income in the year they are paid in most tax jurisdictions.
