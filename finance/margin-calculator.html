Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Markup/Margin:

Calculates retail margins and markups based on cost and sales price:
\(\text{Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{Cost}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100\)
\(\text{Markup \%} = \frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{Cost}}{\text{Cost}} \times 100\)

Step-by-Step Calculation Examples

Review these examples to see how the mathematical formula is applied to different input values:

Example Scenario 1

An item costing $60 sold for $100: Margin = (100 - 60) / 100 = 40%. Markup = (100 - 60) / 60 = 66.67%.

Example Scenario 2

An item costing $15 sold for $20: Margin = (20 - 15) / 20 = 25%. Markup = (20 - 15) / 15 = 33.33%.

Q: What is gross margin?

A: Gross margin is the percentage of sales revenue left over after subtracting the cost of goods sold.

Q: Why is margin lower than markup?

A: Margin is calculated relative to the higher retail sales price, while markup is calculated relative to the lower cost price.
