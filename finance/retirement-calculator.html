Q: What is the 4% rule?

A: A rule of thumb stating you can safely withdraw 4% of your portfolio's value in the first year of retirement, adjusting for inflation in later years, without running out of money.

Q: How does inflation affect my retirement savings?

A: Inflation reduces your buying power over time, meaning you will need a larger retirement nest egg to cover future living expenses.
