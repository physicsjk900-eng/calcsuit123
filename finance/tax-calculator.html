Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate VAT/GST Calc:

To add tax to a price:
\(\text{Total Price} = \text{Net Price} \times (1 + \text{Tax Rate})\)
To remove tax (find net price before tax):
\(\text{Net Price} = \frac{\text{Total Price}}{1 + \text{Tax Rate}}\)
Tax Amount = Total Price - Net Price.

Q: What is the difference between VAT and Sales Tax?

A: VAT is collected at each stage of production and is usually included in the shelf price. Sales tax is added only at the final point of purchase by the consumer.

Q: Why is dividing by 1 + tax rate used to remove tax?

A: Dividing by 1 + tax rate reverses the multiplication used to add the tax, ensuring you calculate the correct pre-tax price.
