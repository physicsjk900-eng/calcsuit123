Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Body Fat Calculator:

Estimates body fat percentage using the US Navy Circumference Method:
- Men: \(\text{BF\%} = 86.010 \log_{10}(\text{abdomen} - \text{neck}) - 70.041 \log_{10}(\text{height}) + 36.76\)
- Women: \(\text{BF\%} = 163.205 \log_{10}(\text{waist} + \text{hip} - \text{neck}) - 97.684 \log_{10}(\text{height}) - 78.387\)

A: For men, a healthy range is typically 10-20%. For women, a healthy range is typically 18-28%.

Q: How accurate is the US Navy method?
