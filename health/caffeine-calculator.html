Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Caffeine Logic:

Caffeine clearance is modeled using a standard 5-hour half-life equation:
\(\text{Caffeine}_t = \text{Caffeine}_0 \times (0.5)^{\frac{t}{5}}\)
This tracks residual caffeine levels in milligrams over elapsed time.

Q: How long does caffeine stay in the body?

A: Caffeine has a half-life of 5 hours, but it can take up to 10 hours to clear completely from the bloodstream.

Q: What is a safe daily limit for caffeine?

A: The FDA recommends a maximum of 400 mg of caffeine per day for healthy adults, equivalent to about 4 cups of coffee.
