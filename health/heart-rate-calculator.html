Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate HR Zones:

MaxHR is estimated using the Tanaka equation:
\(\text{MaxHR} = 208 - (0.7 \times \text{Age})\)
Training zones are structured based on MaxHR percentages:
- Zone 1 (Recovery): 50-60% | Zone 2 (Endurance): 60-70% | Zone 3 (Aerobic): 70-80% | Zone 4 (Anaerobic): 80-90% | Zone 5 (Peak): 90-100%
