Q: What are macros?

A: Macronutrients are the three major food groups your body needs in large quantities to produce energy: proteins, carbohydrates, and fats.

Q: Which macro ratio is best for fat loss?

A: There is no single best ratio, but a higher protein ratio (30-40% of total calories) helps preserve muscle mass and keep you full during a diet.
