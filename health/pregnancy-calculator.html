Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Pregnancy Due:

Due date is estimated using Naegele's Rule based on the Last Menstrual Period (LMP):
\(\text{Due Date} = \text{LMP Date} + 7 \text{ Days} - 3 \text{ Months} + 1 \text{ Year}\)
Assuming a standard 280-day (40-week) pregnancy cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does this online calculator perform calculations?

A: The calculations are processed instantly in your browser using standard mathematical formulas. Your data remains private and is never uploaded to external servers.

Q: How accurate is a due date calculator?

A: Due date calculators provide an estimation. Only about 4-5% of babies are born exactly on their estimated due date; most arrive within 2 weeks before or after.

Q: What is gestational age?

A: Gestational age measures how far along the pregnancy is, calculated from the first day of the mother's last menstrual period, not the actual day of conception.
