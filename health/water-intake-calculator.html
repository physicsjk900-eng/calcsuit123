Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Hydration Master:

Baseline fluid intake is calculated as:
\(\text{Water (ml)} = \text{Weight (kg)} \times 35\)
Adjustments add 350-500 ml of water for every 30 minutes of physical activity, with additional margins added for hot climates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does this online calculator perform calculations?

A: The calculations are processed instantly in your browser using standard mathematical formulas. Your data remains private and is never uploaded to external servers.

Q: How much water should I drink daily?

A: A general guideline is 30-35 ml per kg of body weight, adjusted upwards for high activity levels, heat, or sweating.

Q: Can you drink too much water?

A: Yes, consuming excessive water without replacing electrolytes can lead to hyponatremia, a dangerous condition where sodium levels in the blood drop too low.
