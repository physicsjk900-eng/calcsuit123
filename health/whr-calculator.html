Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Waist-to-Hip:

WHR is calculated using circumference measurements:
\(\text{WHR} = \frac{\text{Waist Circumference}}{\text{Hip Circumference}}\)
Visceral fat risk is flagged at WHR ≥ 0.90 for men and WHR ≥ 0.85 for women.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does this online calculator perform calculations?

A: The calculations are processed instantly in your browser using standard mathematical formulas. Your data remains private and is never uploaded to external servers.

Q: Why is WHR better than BMI?

A: WHR measures fat distribution rather than total weight. Excess abdominal fat (visceral fat) is associated with higher health risks than fat stored in other areas.

Q: Where exactly should I measure my waist?

A: Measure midway between your lowest rib and the top of your hip bone, usually just above the belly button.
