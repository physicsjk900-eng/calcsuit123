Q: Why are weight measurements preferred in baking?

A: Dry ingredients like flour can compress. Measuring by weight (grams) is significantly more accurate than volume (cups) which can vary by up to 20%.

Q: Is a dry cup the same as a liquid cup?

A: They measure the same volume, but dry cups are designed to be leveled off, while liquid cups have pour spouts for fluid accuracy.
