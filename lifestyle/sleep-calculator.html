Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Sleep Cycle:

Sleep cycles are calculated in 90-minute blocks:
\(\text{Wake Time} = \text{Bed Time} + (15 \text{ min sleep onset}) + (N \times 90 \text{ minutes})\)
Where \(N\) represents the number of completed sleep cycles (ideally 5 or 6).

Q: What is sleep inertia?

A: The feeling of grogginess and disorientation experienced when waking up in the middle of deep sleep.

Q: How long does a typical sleep cycle last?

A: A complete sleep cycle lasts approximately 90 minutes, moving from light sleep to deep sleep, and ending in REM sleep.
