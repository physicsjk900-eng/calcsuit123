The 50/30/20 Wedding Rule

Most wedding planners recommend the 50/30/20 split for a balanced event. This means 50% goes to the venue and catering (the experience), 30% to photography, music, and decor (the atmosphere), and 20% to miscellaneous costs, attire, and gifts.

"A budget isn't about restriction; it's about making sure your money goes to what matters most to you both."

Calculates total wedding expenses by summing venue, catering, apparel, and entertainment costs:

Step-by-Step Calculation Examples

Review these examples to see how the mathematical formula is applied to different input values:

Example Scenario 1

A wedding with 100 guests. Venue is $5,000, catering is $80/guest, decor is $2,000, and music is $1,500: Total cost = $5,000 + ($80 * 100) + $2,000 + $1,500 = $16,500.

Example Scenario 2

A wedding with 50 guests. Venue is $2,500 and catering is $60/guest: Total cost = $2,500 + ($60 * 50) = $5,500.
