Q: What is the difference between MB/s and Mbps?

A: Megabits per second (Mbps) measures network transfer speeds, while Megabytes per second (MB/s) measures file sizes. 1 Megabyte (MB) equals 8 Megabits (Mb).

Q: Why are my actual download speeds slower than my internet plan?

A: Network congestion, Wi-Fi interference, server upload throttling, and network overhead can cause real-world speeds to be lower than your ISP plan.
