Q: What is the Law of Sines?

A: A trigonometric law stating the ratio of the length of a side to the sine of its opposite angle is constant for all three angles in a triangle.

Q: Can you solve a triangle with only side lengths?

A: Yes, you can solve all angles using the Law of Cosines if the sum of any two sides is greater than the third side.
