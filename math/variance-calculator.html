Formula & Computational Methodology

Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Percent Variance:

Calculates the percentage variance between a new value and a baseline:
\(\text{Percent Variance} = \frac{\text{New Value} - \text{Baseline Value}}{\text{Baseline Value}} \times 100\)
Also solves percentage difference for comparison: \(\text{Diff \%} = \frac{|A - B|}{(A+B)/2} \times 100\).

Q: What does positive versus negative variance mean?

A: A positive variance means the new value is higher than the baseline. A negative variance means the new value is lower.

Q: How does variance differ from percentage difference?

A: Variance compares a new value to an original baseline (direction matters). Percentage difference compares two numbers by dividing their difference by their average.
