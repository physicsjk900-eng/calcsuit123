                     Formula & Computational Methodology
                

                
Understanding the mathematics behind the calculations helps clarify how the inputs relate to the final result. Our calculator processes the formulas instantly in your browser. Below is the equation used to calculate Ohm’s Law:

                

                    Ohm's Law defines the relationship between Voltage (\(V\)), Current (\(I\)), and Resistance (\(R\)):
\(V = I \times R\)
Electrical Power (\(P\)) is calculated as: \(P = V \times I\) or \(P = I^2 \times R\).

Q: What is Ohm's Law?

                        
A: A fundamental law stating that the current passing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage and inversely proportional to the resistance.

                    

                    

                        
Q: What are the standard units of electrical measurement?

                        
A: Voltage is measured in Volts (V), Current in Amperes (A), Resistance in Ohms (Ω), and Power in Watts (W).
