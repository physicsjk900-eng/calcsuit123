Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

At CalcSuit, accessible from https://calcsuit.com/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by CalcSuit and how we use it.

1. Log Files

CalcSuit follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, CalcSuit uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Google Advertising and Analytics

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Visitors may choose to opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. Alternatively, you can opt-out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

CalcSuit's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

6. Local Processing & Data Minimization

A cornerstone of the CalcSuit philosophy is Local-First Processing. Unlike traditional web applications that send your sensitive data (such as financial details or health metrics) to a remote server for processing, our calculators are designed to execute logic directly within your browser's JavaScript engine.

This means that when you calculate your mortgage or track your metabolic rate, the core mathematical operations happen locally on your device. This architectural choice significantly enhances data privacy and ensures that your sensitive inputs are not stored or processed on our internal servers. We only collect anonymized telemetry to improve tool performance and identify bugs.

7. User Data Rights & Global Standards

While CalcSuit is a global platform, we align our privacy practices with the most stringent international standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We believe that privacy is a fundamental human right, not just a legal requirement.

  • Right to be Forgotten: Since we do not maintain persistent user profiles or store personal input data, you are essentially "forgotten" the moment you close your browser tab.
  • Right to Data Portability: Many of our tools include "Download as CSV" or "Copy to Clipboard" features, allowing you to take your results wherever you need them without friction.
  • Transparency: We are committed to notifying our users of any significant changes to our processing logic or third-party partnerships through updates to this policy.
  • No Sale of Data: CalcSuit does not sell, trade, or rent your personal identifiable information to third parties. We are 100% committed to your data sovereignty.

8. Third-Party Integrations & Security

To maintain our platform as a free resource, we partner with industry-leading advertising and analytics providers. These partners may use non-personally identifiable information (such as your browser type or time of visit) to serve relevant content.

We vet every third-party script for security and performance. Our site is delivered over a secure, encrypted HTTPS connection, providing a robust layer of protection for the tools you use.

9. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. CalcSuit does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
